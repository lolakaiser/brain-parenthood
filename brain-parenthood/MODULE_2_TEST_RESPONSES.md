## Part 2: Goals (3 questions)

### Q1 — Conflict Resolution Approach (text input, non-empty)
**Answer:**
> Pause for three seconds and ask a clarifying question before responding when I feel my defenses rising in a disagreement.

### Q2 — Anger Management Strategy (textarea, non-empty)
**Answer:**
> When I feel anger building — especially in meetings — I will use the physical reset technique: excuse myself briefly, take five slow breaths, and return to the conversation only when I can engage from a place of curiosity rather than defensiveness. I will also keep a short daily log of moments where I felt reactive, noting what triggered it and what I would do differently, so I can spot patterns over time.

### Q3 — Coping Strategy (text input, non-empty)
**Answer:**
> Take a 10-minute walk outside immediately after receiving bad news or a major setback before responding to anyone.
